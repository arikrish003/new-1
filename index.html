About Archers Global Traders

Wholesale • Retail • Branding

Welcome to Archers Global Traders, focused on Wholesale, Retail and Branding. Based in the agricultural heartland of Kudavasal, Thiruvarur District, Tamil Nadu, we specialize in sourcing and distributing 100% pure, farm-fresh spices and dry fruits.

Direct From Farms

We procure raw cardamom, black pepper, and nuts directly from Western Ghats plantations and certified auctions.

Wholesale & Retail

Supplying premium grades to bulk wholesalers, retail shops, and individual households across India with transparent pricing.
