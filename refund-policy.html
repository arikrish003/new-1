Cancellation & Refund Policy

Guidance for order cancellation, returns, and payment refunds.

Last reviewed: 1 November 2026
Please read this page carefully. For order-specific questions, contact us before completing your purchase.

1. Cancellation requests

If you need to cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible with your order reference. Cancellation may depend on whether processing or dispatch has started.

2. Damaged, incorrect, or missing items

If an order arrives damaged, incorrect, or incomplete, contact us promptly and provide the order reference and clear photographs where relevant so the issue can be reviewed.

3. Refund review

Refund eligibility is assessed based on the circumstances of the order, the applicable consumer rules, and any agreed order-specific terms. We will communicate the outcome and next steps after review.

4. Refund timing

Where a refund is approved, the time taken for the funds to appear may depend on the payment provider and the customer’s bank.

5. Non-delivery or payment issues

If payment was deducted but an order confirmation was not received, contact us with the transaction reference. Do not share your card PIN, password, or OTP.

Before publishing: Please confirm your actual cancellation window, return conditions for food products, and refund rules with your business requirements and applicable law. This page is a starter policy, not legal advice.

6. Contact

Submit requests through the Contact Us page.
