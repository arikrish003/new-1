Privacy Policy

How Archers Global Traders handles information shared with us.

Last reviewed: 1 November 2026
Please read this page carefully. For order-specific questions, contact us before completing your purchase.

1. Information we may collect

When you contact us, place an order, or request a quotation, we may receive details such as your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, and order information.

2. How information is used

We use information to respond to enquiries, process and fulfil orders, coordinate delivery, provide customer support, and maintain business records.

3. Payments

Online payments are handled through the payment service provider shown at checkout. We do not ask customers to send card PINs, passwords, or one-time passwords to us.

4. Sharing and security

Information may be shared with service providers only when needed to support payment, delivery, hosting, or customer service. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Cookies and third-party services

Website hosting, analytics, messaging, and payment services may use their own technologies and privacy practices.

6. Contact

For a privacy-related request, contact Archers Global Traders through the Contact Us page.
